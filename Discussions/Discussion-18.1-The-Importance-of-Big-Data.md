# Required Discussion 18.1: The Importance of Big Data

One large technology organization that interests me is **Amazon**. Founded in 1994, Amazon has evolved from an online bookstore into a global leader in e-commerce, cloud computing, logistics, and artificial intelligence. A major change I foresee is the increased use of AI-driven automation across its supply chain, customer service operations, and recommendation systems.

Amazon collects a vast amount of big data, including customer purchases, browsing history, search queries, product reviews, inventory levels, delivery routes, and interactions with services such as Alexa and Amazon Prime Video. This data aligns well with the five V's of big data. The **value** comes from improving customer experiences and business decisions. The **variety** includes structured transaction records, semi-structured logs, and unstructured voice and video data. The **velocity** is extremely high because data is generated continuously by millions of users worldwide. **Veracity** is important because inaccurate data could lead to poor recommendations or inventory decisions. Finally, **volume** is enormous, with billions of transactions and interactions occurring annually.

I would expect Amazon to collect several terabytes or even petabytes of data daily, resulting in massive data growth annually. The organization should retain data for as long as it provides operational, analytical, regulatory, or machine learning value while balancing privacy requirements and storage costs.

Amazon leverages this data to optimize inventory management, personalize product recommendations, forecast customer demand, improve delivery logistics, detect fraudulent activity, and develop new products and services. The insights generated from these datasets create a significant competitive advantage.

Although collecting and storing such data is expensive, it is practical because the business value generated far exceeds the associated costs. Big data enables Amazon to improve operational efficiency, increase revenue, enhance customer satisfaction, and maintain its position as one of the world's leading technology organizations.
