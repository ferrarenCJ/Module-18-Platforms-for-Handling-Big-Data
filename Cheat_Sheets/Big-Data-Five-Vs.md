# Big Data: Five V's Cheat Sheet

## Volume

Amount of data generated and stored.

Examples:

- Terabytes
- Petabytes
- Exabytes

Example Sources:

- Websites
- IoT devices
- Sensors
- Mobile apps

---

## Velocity

Speed at which data is generated and processed.

Examples:

- Streaming data
- Financial transactions
- Sensor telemetry

---

## Variety

Different types of data.

Examples:

- Structured data
- Semi-structured data
- Unstructured data

Formats:

- CSV
- JSON
- XML
- Images
- Video

---

## Veracity

Quality and trustworthiness of data.

Considerations:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Reliability

---

## Value

Business usefulness of data.

Examples:

- Predictive analytics
- Revenue optimization
- Customer insights

---

## Memory Aid

```text
Volume   = Amount
Velocity = Speed
Variety  = Types
Veracity = Quality
Value    = Business Benefit
```
